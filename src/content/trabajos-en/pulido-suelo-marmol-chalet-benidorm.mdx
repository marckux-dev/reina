The floor had not looked like this in years.

## Polishing and crystallisation step by step

The marble showed the wear of years of use: lost shine, scratches and joints that had given way. We worked through four stages:

- Grinding to level and remove accumulated imperfections
- Progressive diamond polishing to open the pores and prepare the surface
- Repair of deteriorated joints
- High-gloss crystallisation for the mirror finish

## The finish that tells the truth

Crystallisation is the hardest to get right. The reflection either comes out clean and even, or it does not. In a villa with marble floors, when it works, it is hard to look away.

Beyond the shine, the crystallisation layer hardens the surface and makes day-to-day cleaning easier without special products.
