After more than 20 years of continuous use, this hotel corridor had lost much of its original appearance. Stains, wear, scratches and an almost complete absence of shine made the floor give off a sense of ageing that was hard to ignore.

## Full terrazzo restoration

Through a complete restoration of the terrazzo — joint repair, diamond polishing and professional crystallisation — we managed to recover the original beauty of the floor and restore the brightness, uniformity and shine that a space like this deserves.

## Terrazzo does not need to be replaced

A good terrazzo does not need to be replaced to look spectacular again. With the right treatment it can recover an appearance that seemed lost, improving the perception of cleanliness, upkeep and quality throughout the whole space.
